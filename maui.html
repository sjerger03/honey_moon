Two Days In

South Maui

Before The Ritz

A menu of options for your two and a half days at Aston Maui Hill before the Ritz check-in at 4 PM Friday, each tagged with a recommended day and time — not a fixed order. Mix and match, and pick just one of the Friday options rather than both.

Beaches

No. 1 · Best Overall

Keawakapu Beach

ActivityBeach
From Aston5 min walk
Best TimeWed afternoon → sunset
Duration1–2 hrs

The best overall beach experience within easy reach — long, sandy, and beautiful, without feeling like you're sitting in the middle of a resort complex. Good for swimming and relaxing through the day, and it connects straight into the Wailea coastal path for sunset.

No. 2 · Best For Snorkeling

Maluaka — Turtle Town

ActivityBeach
From Aston15–20 min drive
Best TimeThu, after diving (optional)
DurationFlexible

The pick if snorkeling and turtles are the priority — swimmable from shore, with a real chance of spotting honu around the reef. Makes sense as a follow-on to Thursday's scuba/snorkel morning if there's still energy for more water time; skip it without guilt if the diving already covered it, and just enjoy Keawakapu or Ulua instead.

Wailea Beach & Coastal Walk

ActivityBeach + walk
From Aston15 min drive · ~35 min walk
Best TimeSunset, ~6:26 PM
Duration45–60 min

A wide resort beach with a paved path running along it that connects straight to Ulua/Mōkapu and back to Keawakapu — no car required if you'd rather walk the whole coastal path, just a longer stretch than the beaches closer to Aston. Best around sunset, roughly 6:26 PM in mid-September, which also puts you within easy reach of dinner in Wailea Village.

Ulua & Mōkapu

ActivityBeach
From Aston15 min drive · ~20 min walk
Best TimeAny time
DurationFlexible

Two connected coves right next to each other, both easy and close — a reliable default beach when you don't want to think about where to go. No car needed either: it's the near end of the paved coastal path that starts at Keawakapu, so you can walk the whole way.

Makena — Big Beach

ActivityBeach
From Aston20–25 min drive
Best TimeMorning · calmer
DurationFlexible

The widest, most undeveloped beach in the area — a rawer stretch of coastline than the manicured resort shoreline further north, and a good complement to the volcanic Big Island scenery you'll already be seeing. Mornings are noticeably calmer and less crowded than midday.

La Perouse Bay

ActivityLava coastline
From Aston35–45 min drive
Best TimeEarly AM or late PM
Duration30–60 min

Where Maui's most recent lava flow, from around 1790, meets the ocean — black rock rather than sand, raw and mostly empty compared to everywhere else you'll stay on this trip. This is a noticeably longer drive than the other South Maui beaches above, so it's worth it specifically if you want to see that rugged volcanic coastline — easy to skip if you'd rather keep things close for these first two days. There's no shade and it gets genuinely hot by midday, so early morning or late afternoon is more comfortable if you do go.

ʻĀhihi-Kīnaʻu Reserve

ActivityNature reserve
From Aston25–30 min drive
Best TimeCheck conditions first
Duration30–60 min

A protected natural area reserve over more recent lava flows, with a small cove used for snorkeling when ocean conditions allow. Its entrance comes first along the same road as La Perouse, a bit further south, so it makes sense to pair the two if you're making the trip rather than visiting separately. Much of the reserve is closed to foot traffic to let the terrain recover — stick to the marked trail and posted areas.

Food

South Maui Fish Company

Rating4.7 ★ Google
ActivitySeafood counter
From Aston~8 min drive
Best TimeWed–Sat only
DurationFlexible

A small, casual seafood counter in Kihei working straight off whatever came in that day — poke, fish tacos, grilled fish plates, and fresh fish to take home. They only operate Wednesday through Saturday, which lines up with the Wed–Fri stay at Aston, though they're known to sell out early depending on the catch. A poke bowl works as an eat-now lunch; a couple pieces of fresh fish can go back to the condo for the grill, if there is one, alongside a bagged salad and rice from Foodland.
