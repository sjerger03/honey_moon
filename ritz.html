Haleakalā & Upcountry

ActivityScenic drive
From Ritz~1.5 hr drive
Best TimeMorning · clearer skies
DurationHalf day
Dive RuleNot before Fri 4:15 AM

Kula's rolling farmland leads up into Haleakalā National Park, where a paved road climbs all the way to the 10,023-foot summit — you can walk the rim of an enormous volcanic crater with surreal red and green cinder cones without any hiking at all. Summit views are usually clearest in the morning before afternoon clouds build up.
