Lahaina Lunch

Friday Lunch — Lahaina

The drive up from Aston Maui Hill to the Ritz runs straight through Lahaina, so Friday's check-in day is the natural time for lunch there. Ranked below by what each is best for, not by order along the drive.

Best For Authentic Plate Lunch

Kalei's

Rating4.3 ★ Google
ActivityPlate lunch diner
LocationLahaina Cannery Mall
From Aston~45 min drive
Duration30–45 min

The top pick if what you want is a proper Hawaiian plate lunch — a sit-down diner rather than a truck, open all day, with the classics done well.

Best For Smaller & Creative

Aloha Braised

Rating4.9 ★ Google
ActivityFood truck
LocationSouth Lahaina
From Aston~40 min drive
Duration20–30 min

A smaller, more inventive braised-meat menu than a standard plate lunch spot — good if you'd rather have something a little different than the usual. Weekdays only, 10 AM–2 PM, so Friday works.

Best Old-School Hidden Gem

Honokowai Okazuya

Rating4.5 ★ Google
ActivityOkazuya deli
LocationHonokowai
From Aston~55 min drive
Duration20–30 min

A no-frills, cash-only local deli that's been doing Japanese-Hawaiian plate food the same way for years — the kind of place locals actually eat at rather than a tourist stop. Farthest north of the five, closest to the Ritz.

Best For Fresh Fish & Poke

The Fish Market Maui

Rating4.5 ★ Google
ActivitySeafood counter
LocationHonokowai
From Aston~55 min drive
Duration20–30 min

Local fishermen bring the day's catch straight here — the pick if fresh fish or poke is what you're after over a plate lunch. Right next door to Honokowai Okazuya, so easy to compare the two on the spot.

Best For Oceanfront Setting

Aloha Mixed Plate

Rating4.5 ★ Google
ActivityPlate lunch, oceanfront
LocationFront St, Lahaina
From Aston~45 min drive
Duration45–60 min

The pick if the setting matters as much as the food — tables right on the water on Lahaina's Front Street. Reopened on the original waterfront site after the 2023 fires.
